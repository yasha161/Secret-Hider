Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 18, 2026

🔒
Short version:

Your messages, images, and secrets never leave your device. We don't collect them, we can't read them, and we have no server to send them to. This app is built for fun and personal privacy — not for harvesting your data.

👋 Who We Are

Secret Hider is a free steganography tool that lets you hide secret messages inside text, emojis, and images. It's available as an Android app on Google Play and via Google AI Studio. It was built by an independent developer based in India, purely for fun and for people who care about their privacy.

🚫 What We Do NOT Collect

We keep this simple, because there's genuinely nothing to report:

  • We do not collect, transmit, store, or process the messages, images, or any content you encode or decode.
  • Everything — encoding, decoding, encryption — happens entirely on your device. Your secrets never travel to any server.
  • We do not require you to create an account or log in.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any personal identifying information to use the core tool.
Zero-knowledge by design. Even if we wanted to read your hidden messages, we architecturally cannot — because they never leave your device.

🗄️ Vault & Local Storage

The Secret Vault feature saves your hidden messages locally on your device, inside the app's private storage. This data is stored only on your device and never synced, uploaded, or transmitted anywhere.

If you uninstall the app or clear app data, this locally stored data will be permanently deleted. We have no copy of it and cannot help you recover it, so back up anything you care about keeping.

📊 Analytics & Advertising (Currently: None)

At this time, Secret Hider does not use any analytics services or advertising networks.

If we add these in the future (for example, anonymous page-view analytics or ads to keep the app free), this policy will be updated before those services go live, and we will clearly disclose what data those third-party services may collect. Any such data would be anonymous and aggregate — things like page views, general region, or device type — never your message content.

💡 We'll always give you a heads-up before anything changes. No surprises.

🍪 Cookies

Secret Hider does not currently use cookies. If third-party analytics or advertising is added in the future, those services may use cookies or similar tracking technologies. You can always control or disable cookies in your browser settings.

🧒 Children's Privacy

Secret Hider is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we'll take appropriate steps.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the app or website after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
