What is this?

Secret Hider is a free tool that lets you hide secret messages where no one would think to look — tucked invisibly inside a normal sentence, hiding behind a single emoji, or embedded in the pixels of a photo.

It's real steganography, made approachable. No PhD required.

💡

Why we built it

Not everything needs to be a business secret or a classified document. Sometimes you just want to:

  • Slip a secret compliment into a text
  • Send a surprise message disguised as a boring work update
  • Hide an inside joke inside a photo for your friend to find
  • Keep a private note that looks like nothing at all

We built Secret Hider for people — not corporations. It's for fun, privacy, personal moments, and the joy of having a secret.

⚙️

How it works (the short version)

📝

Hide in Text

Your secret message gets converted into invisible zero-width characters that hide between the words of a normal-looking sentence. The text looks completely unchanged to anyone who reads it.

🤫

Hide in Emoji

Your secret is embedded inside a standard emoji using invisible Unicode Tag characters. The emoji looks 100% normal. The secret is invisibly attached.

🖼️

Hide in Image

We use LSB (Least Significant Bit) steganography to flip tiny pixel bits in a photo's RGB channels. The image looks identical to the naked eye. Your secret is in the pixels.

All three methods support optional password protection (XOR encryption), so even if someone finds the hidden payload, they'd still need the key.

🔒 The promise: your data stays with you

Nothing leaves your device. No uploads, no servers, no logging of your messages. We never see what you hide — because it never comes to us. It's genuinely private.

🎉

Free, forever

No account. No subscription. No "premium tier." Secret Hider is free and always will be.
