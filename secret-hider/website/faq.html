Frequently Asked Questions 💬

Real answers to real questions. No corporate fluff.

Is this actually secure? Can someone else find my hidden message?

The hiding technique (steganography) makes your message invisible to anyone who doesn't know to look for it. A normal person receiving your text or emoji won't see anything unusual — it just looks like ordinary text or an emoji.

If you also set a password, even someone who knows to look and has the right tool would still need your password to read the message. That said, we use XOR encryption, not AES-256, so treat this as strong privacy, not military-grade cryptography. Don't use it to protect nuclear codes 😁

Do you store or see my messages?

No. Full stop. Everything — encoding, decoding, encryption, storage — happens entirely on your device. Your messages never travel to any server. We have no way to see them even if we wanted to.

Can I use this to prank or surprise a friend? 🎉

Absolutely — that's basically why it exists! Send a boring "hey check this out" text that secretly says "Happy Birthday!" Send a random emoji that has a secret message hiding inside it. Hide a treasure hunt clue inside a photo. Have fun with it.

Is this legal?

Yes, for personal and lawful use. Steganography itself is completely legal. Using Secret Hider to hide a birthday message, keep a private note, or share an inside joke is totally fine.

Just don't use it for fraud, scams, harassment, or anything illegal — that's on you, not us. See our Terms of Service for details.

Will the hidden message survive copy-pasting into WhatsApp or Instagram?

It depends on the method:

Hide in Text 📝 — Works great with WhatsApp, Discord, iMessage, and most chat apps. They preserve the invisible zero-width characters in plain text.

Hide in Emoji 🤫 — Also survives copy-paste in most apps.

Hide in Image 🖼️ — Requires sharing as a lossless PNG file. If WhatsApp re-compresses the image (lossy JPEG), the hidden data will be destroyed. Use the direct Share button in the app to send the PNG file directly without re-compression.

What happens if I clear app data or uninstall — do I lose my Vault?

Yes. The Secret Vault stores your saved messages locally on your device. If you clear the app's data or uninstall it, that data is permanently deleted. We can't recover it because it was only ever on your device. Export or note down anything important before clearing data.

Is it really free? Do I need to sign up?

It's genuinely free. No sign-up, no account, no email, no subscription. Just open the app and use it. We don't collect any personal information to use the core tool.

What's the difference between the 3 methods? Which should I use?

Hide in Text 📝 — Most versatile. Works in any chat that handles text. The carrier message can be any normal sentence. Best for most everyday use.

Hide in Emoji 🤫 — Most playful. Your entire secret bundles into 1–3 emojis. Perfect for short secrets or vibe-coded messages.

Hide in Image 🖼️ — Highest capacity (an 800×800 image can hold tens of thousands of characters) and most visually natural, but requires sharing a lossless PNG file. Best for longer secrets or when sharing a photo fits naturally.
