Modern chat apps are constantly monitored by curious friends, siblings, and coworkers looking over shoulders. Standard end-to-end encryption protects your messages from network hackers, but it does nothing to stop someone reading right off your screen!

Enter Zero-Width Message Infiltration: a clever trick that packs hidden binary data into everyday chat messages without breaking the display of WhatsApp, Instagram, or Discord.

How Does It Work on Social Apps?

WhatsApp and Instagram are designed to handle millions of international languages and complex emojis. To do this, their text engines support Unicode formatting characters, including zero-width spaces and tag characters.

When you use Secret Hider, your secret message is converted into these exact invisible Unicode characters. Because they take up 0 pixels of width, WhatsApp displays your normal carrier sentence completely unaltered!
